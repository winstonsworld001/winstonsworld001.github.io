Geode Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version. Geode has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your collection stays on your phone. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to an analytics service so we can see what people dig and whether they come back. This version shows no ads. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Geode is a rock collecting game for iPhone made by an independent developer. In this policy "we" means the developer of Geode. You can reach us at winstonsworld001@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: the rocks waiting in your tray, your cabinet and its arrangement, the pieces on sale, your field guide, the journal of visits, your money and upgrades, and your sound and vibration settings. The photos you take of your cabinet or a piece are written to the app's own folder; the game never reads your camera roll. It also keeps a small technical log that helps diagnose crashes. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game sends

Geode uses GameAnalytics to measure how the game is played. It receives:

  • gameplay events, such as rocks opened, pieces kept or sold, photos shared, claims opened and upgrades bought;
  • session information, such as when a session starts and how long it lasts;
  • device and app details, such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate country;
  • a random identifier created for the app, and your device's identifier for this developer's apps, so sessions from the same install can be counted together;
  • error and crash reports, so we can fix what breaks.

This data is not linked to your name or any account, and we use it only to improve the game: tuning the dig, finding bugs, and measuring how many players return. GameAnalytics processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy, linked above.

Sharing

When you share a photo or a code, the game hands it to your phone's own share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is up to you and leaves through the app you choose. A share code is eight characters that describe a specimen; it contains nothing about you.

Advertising

This version of Geode shows no ads. We may advertise Geode on other platforms, such as Meta. Apple measures those ad campaigns with its SKAdNetwork system, which reports anonymous install counts to the ad platform. The game itself shares nothing with them. If a later version adds ads, this page will say so before it is released.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or health data.

Children

Geode is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can stop all data collection by deleting the app. You can also email us to ask what analytics data exists for your install or to have it deleted. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you further rights, including access, correction and deletion. We will answer any such request within 30 days.

How long we keep data

Analytics data is kept only as long as it is useful for improving the game, and no longer than GameAnalytics' standard retention period.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version is released.
