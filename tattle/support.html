Help with the puzzle game Tattle Tails for iPhone and iPad

Need a hand? Email winstonsworld001@gmail.com. Tell us your device model, your iOS version and what happened, and we will get back to you within a few days.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Tattle Tails has no accounts and does not sync between devices, so deleting the app or switching phones starts the game over.

How do I start over?

Open the menu, tap the gear in the top corner, then tap Reset progress twice. Your sound and vibration settings are kept.

How does the daily puzzle work?

There is a new daily puzzle every day at midnight, your local time, the same board for everyone. You can solve it once a day, and hints are switched off for it. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.

How do I get more hints?

You start with 3. You earn one for each day you open the game and one for solving the daily puzzle. You can hold up to 9 at a time. When you have none left, you can watch a short ad for one.

The rules in short

A board has 20 creatures and 8 of them are thieves. Tap a creature, then tap Friend or Thief. Each creature you mark can give a clue. Tap a clue to see its squares on the board. The game accepts a mark only when the clues prove it. Every creature tells the truth, also a thief.

Hearts and lives

Each board has 3 hearts. Each mark that the clues do not prove costs 1 heart. With no hearts, the board is over. To try the board again costs 1 of your 5 lives. One life comes back every 30 minutes. You can also watch a short video for a heart or a life. The daily puzzle costs no lives.

The game does not accept my mark

The clues do not prove that mark yet. Read the clues again, or tap Hint. Hint shows which clues to read, then which creature the clues prove, then its answer. To leave a board, tap Menu. The game saves your board.

Sound or vibration

Both can be turned off in Settings, under the gear on the menu. Tattle Tails follows your iPhone's silent switch and plays alongside your own music.
