Help with the puzzle game Koiro for iPhone

Need a hand? Email winstonsworld001@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we will get back to you within a few days.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Koiro has no accounts and does not sync between devices, so deleting the app or switching phones starts the game over.

How do I start over?

Open the menu, tap the gear in the top corner, then tap Reset progress twice. Your sound and vibration settings are kept.

How does the daily pond work?

There is a new daily pond every day at midnight, your local time, the same pond for everyone. You can solve it once a day, and hints are switched off for it. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.

How do I get more hints?

You start with 3. You earn one for each day you open the game and one for solving the daily pond. You can hold up to 9 at a time. When you have none left, you can watch a short ad for one.

The rules in short

Tap a tile and an orange koi swims in. Tap the tile again and the koi becomes white. Tap again and the koi swims away. No more than two koi of the same color can be next to each other in a row or column. Each row and each column has the same number of orange koi and white koi. The = sign between two tiles means the two koi have the same color. The × sign means they have different colors. You cannot change koi with lilies. Put a koi on each tile to complete the pond.

I made a wrong move

Tap Undo to take back changes one at a time, including a whole drag. To restart a pond, tap Menu and choose Start again.

Sound or vibration

Both can be turned off in Settings, under the gear on the menu. Koiro follows your iPhone's silent switch and plays alongside your own music.
