Help with the puzzle game Tumblio for iPhone

Need a hand? Email winstonsworld001@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we will get back to you within a few days.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Tumblio has no accounts and does not sync between devices, so deleting the app or switching phones starts the game over.

How do I start over?

Open the menu, tap the gear in the top corner, then tap Reset progress twice. Your sound and vibration settings are kept.

How does the daily puzzle work?

There is a new daily puzzle every day at midnight, your local time. You can solve it once, and hints are switched off for it. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.

How do I get more hints?

You start with 3. You earn one for each day you open the game, one for finishing a level in par moves or fewer, and one for solving the daily puzzle. You can hold up to 9 at a time.

I made a wrong move

Tap Undo to take back moves one at a time. To restart a level, tap Menu and choose Reset level.

Sound or vibration

Both can be turned off in Settings, under the gear on the menu. Tumblio follows your iPhone's silent switch and plays alongside your own music.
