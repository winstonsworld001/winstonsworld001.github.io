Help with the stained glass puzzle game Tracery for iPhone and iPad

Need a hand? Email winstonsworld001@gmail.com. Tell us your device model, your iOS version and what happened, and we will get back to you within a few days.

The rules in short

Drag across the grid to cut a pane of glass. Each pane must hold exactly one jewel. The number on a jewel is how many squares its pane covers. The cut of the jewel is the shape of its pane: square, wide, tall, or any shape. The panes fill the whole window with no gaps. Every window has one answer, and logic alone finds it.

The game does not accept my pane

A pane that breaks a rule falls away: it covers no jewel or two jewels, or it has the wrong size or shape for its jewel. A new pane that overlaps panes you already set replaces them. Tap a pane to remove it, or tap Undo.

How do hints work?

If a pane you set is wrong, Hint marks it with a crack. If all your panes are right, Hint shows the next pane as frosted glass and tells you why it must go there. You start with 3 hints. You earn one for each day you open the game and one for solving the daily window. You can hold up to 9 at a time. When you have none left, you can watch a short ad for one.

How does the daily window work?

There is a new daily window every day at midnight, your local time, the same window for everyone. It is easiest on Monday and hardest on Sunday. Hints are off for it. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak. Copy puts a short text picture of your window on the clipboard so you can share it.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone or iPad. Tracery has no accounts and does not sync between devices, so deleting the app or switching phones starts the game over.

How do I start over?

Open the menu, tap the gear in the top corner, then tap Reset progress twice. Your sound and vibration settings are kept.

Sound or vibration

Both can be turned off in Settings, under the gear on the menu. Tracery follows your iPhone's silent switch and plays alongside your own music.
