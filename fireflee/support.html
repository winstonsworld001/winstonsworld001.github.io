Help with the puzzle game Fireflee for iPhone

Need a hand? Email winstonsworld001@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we will get back to you within a few days.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Fireflee has no accounts and does not sync between devices, so deleting the app or switching phones starts the game over.

How do I start over?

Open the menu, tap the gear in the top corner, then tap Reset progress twice. Your sound and vibration settings are kept.

How does the daily night work?

There is a new daily night every day at midnight, your local time, the same garden for everyone. You can solve it once a day, and hints are switched off for it. Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak.

How do I get more hints?

You start with 3. You earn one for each day you open the game and one for solving the daily night. You can hold up to 9 at a time. When you have none left, you can watch a short ad for one.

The rules in short

Double tap a tile to place a firefly. Its light runs along its row and column until it reaches a stone. Fireflies are shy: never put two in each other's light. A numbered stone has exactly that many fireflies beside it. Light every tile to finish the night. Tap a tile once to cross it off, or drag across several.

I made a wrong move

Tap Undo to take back changes one at a time, including a whole drag. To restart a night, tap Menu and choose Reset night.

Sound or vibration

Both can be turned off in Settings, under the gear on the menu. Fireflee follows your iPhone's silent switch and plays alongside your own music.
