Fireflee Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version. Fireflee has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress stays on your phone. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to an analytics service so we can see which nights are too hard and whether people come back. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives the device and ad details it needs to serve them. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Fireflee is a puzzle game for iPhone made by an independent developer. In this policy "we" means the developer of Fireflee. You can reach us at winstonsworld001@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: the night you have reached, hints used per night, finished lessons, daily night and login streaks, hint tokens, and your sound and vibration settings. It also keeps a small technical log that helps diagnose crashes. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game sends

Fireflee uses GameAnalytics to measure how the game is played. It receives:

  • gameplay events, such as nights started and finished, hints and undo used, lessons completed, and daily nights played;
  • session information, such as when a session starts and how long it lasts;
  • device and app details, such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate country;
  • a random identifier created for the app, and your device's identifier for this developer's apps, so sessions from the same install can be counted together;
  • error and crash reports, so we can fix what breaks.

This data is not linked to your name or any account, and we use it only to improve the game: tuning difficulty, finding bugs, and measuring how many players return. GameAnalytics processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy, linked above.

Advertising

Fireflee shows ads served by Google AdMob: a short ad between some nights, and an ad you can choose to watch for a hint when you have none left.

To serve ads, limit how often they repeat, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK in the game receives:

  • device and app details, such as device model, iOS version, app version and language;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as ad load times and crashes in the ad SDK;
  • your device's identifier for this developer's apps.

Fireflee does not ask for permission to track, so iOS does not give Google your device's advertising identifier. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We may advertise Fireflee on other platforms, such as Meta. Apple measures those ad campaigns with its SKAdNetwork system, which reports anonymous install counts to the ad platform. The game itself shares nothing with them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or health data.

Children

Fireflee is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can stop all data collection by deleting the app. Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone controls whether apps may ask to track you. You can also email us to ask what analytics data exists for your install or to have it deleted. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you further rights, including access, correction and deletion. We will answer any such request within 30 days.

How long we keep data

Analytics data is kept only as long as it is useful for improving the game, and no longer than GameAnalytics' standard retention period.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version is released.
